About

Design that connects.

I'm Katie, a creative professional with experience across graphic design, digital content, social media and marketing.

I currently lead creative and digital work for FC Copa Academy and Performance Sports Center, where my role ranges from campaign design and social content to photography, email marketing and web updates. My background also includes professional baseball and NCAA athletics, where I developed a strong understanding of fast-paced creative environments and audience-focused content.

I enjoy work that combines strong design with a clear purpose. Whether I'm developing a campaign, building a brand system or creating content for digital platforms, I like figuring out how the pieces work together and how creative decisions support the larger goal.
