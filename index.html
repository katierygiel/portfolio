About

A background in sports. A career in creative.

I’m a graphic designer and digital marketer with a background in competitive athletics and sports marketing. My work spans campaign design, social media, brand development, photography, and visual storytelling.

Currently, I create branded marketing content for FC Copa, developing creative assets that support club initiatives, athlete recognition, events, and digital communications.

Previously, I worked with the New York Boulders and Ramapo College Athletics, creating content for professional and collegiate sports.

I’m passionate about combining thoughtful design with the energy of sports and culture to create work that is both visually compelling and strategically effective.
