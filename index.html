About Me

Design that connects.

I'm a creative professional based in New Jersey with experience across graphic design, digital content, social media, brand work, marketing, photography, and creative production. Right now I lead all marketing and creative for FC Copa Academy and Performance Sports Center, where I handle everything from design and content to email, website, and campaign support.

Before that I spent time in professional baseball with the New York Boulders and two and a half years in NCAA Division III athletics at Ramapo College, where I produced over 300 graphics across more than ten varsity programs.

I'm comfortable moving between creative execution and broader marketing needs. I can go from designing a campaign graphic to writing copy, building a content calendar, or putting together a presentation. I work well independently, I can collaborate with a team, and I know how to manage multiple projects without things falling apart.

Sports is a big part of my background and I enjoy that work. I'm also interested in roles outside sports where strong creative and digital skills are the priority.
